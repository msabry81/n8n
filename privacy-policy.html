Privacy Policy

For the n8n project · Effective date: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit this n8n project website or use workflows configured by the project operator. This is an independent project and is not the official website of n8n GmbH or TikTok.

1. Information collected by this website

This static information website does not provide user registration, account profiles, contact forms, or payment processing. The website itself is not designed to request personal information directly. GitHub Pages or your browser may process standard technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, according to the hosting provider's policies.

2. Information processed by automation workflows

If you use or authorize an automation workflow, the workflow may process information needed for its configured functions. Depending on the configuration, this may include account identifiers, authorization tokens, video files, captions, scheduling details, and publishing status. The exact information processed depends on the workflow and the permissions you grant to connected services.

3. How information is used

Workflow information is used only to operate, troubleshoot, secure, and maintain the configured automation and its connected services. Do not connect an account or submit information unless you understand and accept the workflow's configuration.

4. Storage, sharing, and retention

Workflow data may be stored in the n8n instance, its configured database, execution logs, or other storage selected by the operator. Access to data and retention periods depend on those settings. Data may be transmitted to a third-party platform when necessary to perform an action you have authorized. This project does not sell personal information.

5. Third-party services

Connected platforms process information under their own privacy policies and terms. You should review those policies before authorizing access. This project does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers.

6. Security

The operator should protect credentials, restrict access to the automation instance, use secure connections, and configure suitable execution-log retention. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and revoking access

You can stop using the workflow and revoke its permissions through the connected platform's account settings where available. If the operator controls the workflow instance, contact that operator to request information about stored workflow data or its deletion. Revoking a token may not automatically remove previously stored logs or exported files.

8. Children's privacy

This project is not intended for children and does not knowingly request personal information from children through this website.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or workflow behavior changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project operator through the contact method associated with the account or repository that provided you access to this workflow. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other secrets in a support request.

Before publishing: Confirm that these statements match your actual n8n hosting, workflow data, execution-log settings, and connected services. Add a monitored contact email or contact page if the reviewing platform requires one.
